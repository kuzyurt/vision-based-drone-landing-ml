# Experiment records

Frozen records of the experiments from October and November 2026: code
snapshots, weights, receipts and results. They are kept because the benchmark
and the final policy refer to some of them by path and hash. Nothing in here
describes the current policy; that is in
[`landing_training/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`](../landing_training/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md#versions).

The few notes left in here (`RESULTS.md`, `README.md`) sit inside sealed policy
packages, whose hash lists include them. Their status claims belong to the day
they were written.
