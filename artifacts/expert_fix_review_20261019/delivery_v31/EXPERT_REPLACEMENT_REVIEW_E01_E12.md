# Replacement expert videos — E01–E12

These primary videos show the observable expert driving in MuJoCo, with no radio. They contain actual newly flown onboard and overview recordings; the footage has not been stabilized, warped, or cosmetically corrected. Physical camera disturbances are labelled separately. All recordings remain **review only / excluded from training**.

E09–E11 are explicit **expert-only demonstrations in the same original scenarios**, replacing the old mixed learner/teacher videos. They show how the repaired teacher drives; they are not claimed as observed learner rescues. E04a is a close-up window from E04, not another independent flight.

Full flights are time-compressed to about 30 seconds; the additional alignment and fault windows retain normal speed when their source duration fits. The playback multiplier and actual task clock appear in every video.

Source and command audits, exact capture joins, source hashes and per-video playback/SHA receipts are included in this folder. These developmental demonstrations do not establish policy reliability.
