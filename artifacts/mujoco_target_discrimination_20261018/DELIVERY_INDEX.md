# Reviewable experiment outputs

Start with [the results report](EXPERIMENT_RESULTS_20261019.md). It records the
achievements, failed qualification, limited baseline comparison and remaining work.

- `vm_results/experimental_evidence.zip`: experimental checkpoint, frozen sources,
  scenarios, compact audits and failure-camera sheets. **Not qualified for deployment.**
- `vm_results/local_experimental_archive_verified.json`: local SHA/size/ZIP checks.
- `vm_results/v25_actual_loss_recovery_20s.mp4`: actual delivered camera during the
  new candidate's local recovery. This window ends before the final touchdown.
- `vm_results/original_r15_false_cross_20s.mp4`: original baseline approaching a
  copied-cross board. Native full-flight outcome is collision failure.
- `vm_results/baseline_collision_actual_rgb.jpg`: recorded baseline sequence.
- `NEXT_ITERATION_CONTRACT.md`: concrete requirements for the missing search and
  transition behaviour, before another large collection or training run.

Old recordings, source revisions, rejected previews, checkpoints and failed
experiments remain preserved. `STATUS.md` is the chronological operational log;
the numbered subplans and source scripts are supporting engineering evidence.
