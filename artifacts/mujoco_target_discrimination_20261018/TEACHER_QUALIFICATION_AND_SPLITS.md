# Teacher qualification and data separation

Preparation only, 19 October 2026. Human review is pending. This document launches
no collection, grants no eligibility and does not replace the failed static gate.

## What must be established

The existing recognizer missed almost half of cold known-design views at its
selected conservative operating point. Temporal memory permits successful flights,
but selected successes do not establish a dependable teacher. Keep the original
227/452 result and failed 80% static gate in every qualification receipt.

Measure three separate questions before deciding whether the teacher can supply
useful data: does it eventually acquire a recognizable target; does it falsely
confirm a distractor; does its physical inspection/recovery remain safe? A high
landing rate cannot erase false identity assertions, and a safe timeout does not
count as a boat landing. Do not simply replace the failed static threshold with
a convenient temporal threshold after observing results.

Use the deployed proposal generator and full frame history, not ground-truth
crops or oracle object tracks. Observer labels may score predictions afterward.
Report first recognizable opportunity, time to confirmed identity, time to metric
acquisition, longest stale pursuit, wrong-design confirmations and descent without
supported geometry. Visibility heuristics must be reported as such; frame count
alone is not independent evidence. Group statistics by world and trajectory.

Any future temporal qualification protocol must be frozen before its new cases
run, with explicit tolerances justified against the physical task. Preserve every
case, including absent targets, missing paint, ambiguous views and unsuccessful
acquisition. Existing V14–V18 flights are development evidence, never fresh tests.

## Separation before generating new data

Create an inventory of actual existing world seeds, geometry hashes, distractor
families, illumination seeds and trajectory IDs before choosing new seeds. No
numeric seed range can be called fresh merely because it looks different.

Assign complete worlds and trajectories to train, development or final test.
Keep alternate paint states, crops, camera effects, repeated frames and delayed
deliveries of the same capture in the same split. Group source captures and their
derived features by timestamp plus RGB SHA and record their parent trajectory.
Keep held-out distractor designs separate by source geometry family, rather than
allowing recolored copies to masquerade as unseen boats. The fixed target boat
is intentionally common to all splits: recognition of this design is the task.

Inventory prior exposed development worlds and external Tidewater cases. Exclude
these from a new fresh native test. Preserve the earlier untouched static split;
do not use it as iterative teacher tuning data. Freeze the final 60-case native
suite and 10-case fallback suite independently of training and development.
Tidewater remains external evaluation after native candidate selection is frozen.

## Collection and training receipts

Require separate, explicit evidence for the inherited qualified environment,
new observable teacher/source revision, approved curriculum and human preview
judgment. A receipt must identify which scope it covers. Do not modify historic
approvals, fabricate reviewed episodes or flip review recordings to eligible.
Resolve compatibility with the actual gate before launching any collector.

Freeze hashes of actor dependencies, recognizer, original pose/spatial models,
observer, recording hooks, compiled scene and plan. Assert the actual cloned
runtime bindings. Record exact capture/delivery image provenance and action owner.
The observer must not return labels to the actor or alter control decisions.

Failed or assisted flights may contain useful perception observations, but they
are not successful learner demonstrations. Admit labels only through explicit
visibility and ownership masks plus approved provenance. A fault command is never
a teacher action. Train only applicable learned components; explicit control
improvements require physical evaluation rather than action-imitation claims.

Measure worker throughput on this exact recording/annotation workload after the
required review and technical readiness. Reuse cached worlds, cap compiler
concurrency, and select completed useful observations per wall second, including
memory and disk headroom. Do not extrapolate native flight throughput from the
previous static-render benchmark or start redundant long worker trials.

## Stop conditions

Stop on source mismatch, missing exact image annotation join, actor truth access,
wrong-object touchdown, collision or unsupported descent. Preserve the evidence
and diagnose the specific cause. A missing fault trigger means missing recovery
coverage, not a passed recovery test. Never train on final-test failures and then
report the same suite as fresh. Report incomplete qualification honestly at the
authorized window limit.
