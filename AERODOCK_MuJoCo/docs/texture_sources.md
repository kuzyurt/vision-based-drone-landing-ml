# Harbor facade texture

Project asset: [harbor_facade.png](../assets/textures/harbor_facade.png). Created with the built-in image-generation tool on 2026-10-07, copied unedited from its generated output. Other terrain/facade textures are generated deterministically by `tools/setup_world_assets.py`.

Exact generation prompt:

Asset type: reusable seamless game texture. Create one square photorealistic diffuse/albedo facade texture for a small European harbor town. Perfectly front-on orthographic view of weathered warm beige cream stucco with four regularly spaced columns and four storeys of reasonably large dark blue-gray window glass. Traditional subtle white recessed window frames, muted shutters on some windows, faint realistic wall weathering and plaster grain. Fill the entire square image edge-to-edge with facade: no sky, pavement, roof, perspective, building silhouette, people, text, labels, watermark or harsh shadows. The four outer edges must tile seamlessly so repeated textures make coherent additional window bays/storeys; windows fully inside each repeating unit, evenly spaced. Natural photographic detail, restrained neutral warm colors, soft flat overcast illumination, no dramatic lighting. This texture will be tiled in metric coordinates on lightweight 3D waterfront buildings; visual realism should come from surface detail, with no large painted geometry or lighting gradients.

# Coastal bedrock material

Project asset: [rock_cliff_photo.png](../assets/textures/rock_cliff_photo.png), generated with the built-in image-generation tool and copied unedited. Used with metric 8 m tiling on irregular cliff surfaces. Other rock/wet-rock materials remain deterministic procedural textures.

Exact prompt:

Create one seamless square photorealistic diffuse/albedo game texture of a naturally weathered coastal cliff rock face, representing roughly an 8 metre by 8 metre exposed surface. Gray-brown sedimentary stone, irregular fractured slabs, horizontal stratification broken by diagonal and vertical fissures, sparse dark mineral streaks and salt-weathered pale patches. This is natural bedrock, not brickwork, stone masonry, cobblestones, gravel, stacked rocks, or a wall built by people. Uneven geological layers and organic irregularity, restrained midtone gray and warm brown, no dominant repeating geometric grid. Front-on orthographic material scan with flat overcast illumination, very little baked shadow; all four edges tile seamlessly. Fill the entire image with the rock surface. No sky, water, grass, buildings, large landscape silhouettes, text, frames, labels, or watermark. Surface detail should make a lightweight low-poly coastal cliff look realistic without any extra geometry.
