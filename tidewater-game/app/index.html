TIDEWATER

Pilot a survey drone above Tidewater Bay, keep the radio link clear, and guide the launch boat along its offshore course.

The first launch compiles the shaders and can take a minute or two. Later visits load much faster.
Tip

Move with W A S D, drag the mouse or use the arrow keys to aim the camera, and use R / F to climb or descend.

The on-screen SX1262 readout is simulated from range and terrain line of sight; it is not connected to radio hardware.

Press B to switch between the drone feed and boat helm. Press U at the helm to change boat camera.

The boat follows a closed loop east of the wooden pier, clear of the reef and shoreline.

Press P for automatic/manual boat control, and use the deck buttons to operate the hatches and launch platform.

If the radio link drops, move into range or clear the terrain, then press C to retry the handshake.

Press Home or Reset vehicles to return both craft to their starting positions.
