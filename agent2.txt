1. WHAT THE PROJECT IS
----------------------
A drone (Holybro X500 V2, PX4) finds a moving boat (AERODOCK) from up to 100 m
with its camera and lands on the 60 cm pad. Trained only in MuJoCo with PX4 in
the loop. Neural net = perception only; geometry + rules fly; PX4 does motors.
Tidewater (tidewater-game/) is a browser game used as a visual-transfer test.
