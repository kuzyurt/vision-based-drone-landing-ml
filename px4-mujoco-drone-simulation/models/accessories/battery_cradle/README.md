# Battery cradle for the X500 V2 model

The Lumenier 4S pack (`Body005`) only rested on the top plate. This cradle holds
it and is screwed to the top plate's free M3 holes beside the pack.

| File | Part | Suggested material |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `battery_cradle.obj` | printed cradle: two end walls, low and high side rails with a window between them, a foot at each end with three ribs | light grey nylon, rgba `0.60 0.62 0.65 1` |
| `battery_straps.obj` | two hook-and-loop straps over the pack and around the upper rails | black webbing, rgba `0.045 0.047 0.052 1` |
| `battery_screws.obj` | eight M3 button-head screws with washers, two strap buckles | steel, rgba `0.74 0.75 0.77 1` |

Units are millimetres in the `base_link` frame of `models/flight.xml`
(x forward, z up; the top plate's upper face is z = 32 mm). The pack occupies
x −43.6…42.4, y ±36, z 31.9…73.9 mm.

The screws use the top plate's eight free 3.4 mm holes at x = ±50 mm,
y = ±23 mm and ±37 mm. The feet stop 4 mm short of the arm-clamp screws at
(±58.1, ±42.5) mm; the GPS mast and the propeller discs are clear.

The meshes are generated by `tidewater-game/app/tools/battery_cradle.py`, which
also holds every dimension. Do not edit the OBJ files by hand.

## Where it is used

The Tidewater game shows the cradle in the operator view
(`tidewater-game/app/public/landing/detail/`). The drone's own camera, and with
it the trained landing policy, never sees it.

`models/drone.xml` and `models/flight.xml` are **not** changed: they are
generated from the CAD export and verified against it, and `flight.xml` is the
model the landing policy was trained and benchmarked with. To show the cradle in
MuJoCo, add it as visual-only geoms (no mass, no contact) inside `base_link` of
`flight.xml`:

```xml
<mesh name="battery_cradle" file="../accessories/battery_cradle/battery_cradle.obj" scale=".001 .001 .001" />
...
<geom name="battery_cradle" type="mesh" mesh="battery_cradle" rgba="0.60 0.62 0.65 1"
      contype="0" conaffinity="0" density="0" group="2" />
```

A real cradle of this size printed in PA12 weighs roughly 30 g with its screws;
that mass is not in the mass model.
