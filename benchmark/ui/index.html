How the score is calculated

Landing, wind, waves and combined conditions use strict target-landing success rates. Acquisition uses a correct pad estimate sustained for 0.4 s within 120 s, with no false acquisition or collision. Mission speed gives credit only after a strict target landing, using a shared distance and descent reference.

Incomplete or invalid trials never become zero-valued results. Each policy must finish the full frozen suite before its total is shown. Speed averages the five source categories equally. Observable teachers are separate actors; an assisted learner flight cannot earn an unaided policy score.
